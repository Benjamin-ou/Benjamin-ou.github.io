My featured analysis uses a public sample HR dataset, so its findings show my method, not facts about a real company. The projects below are from my master’s at CNAM and my work at The Capital Group Ltd.

Employee attrition: who leaves, and where should HR focus?

  • Python
  • pandas
  • SQL
  • matplotlib
  • 16.1% of 1,470 employees left.
  • Overtime workers left at 30.5%, against 10.4% for the rest.
  • 52.6% of Level 1 staff on overtime left. That group made up 34.6% of all leavers.
  • The first year was the riskiest: 34.9% attrition, falling to 8.1% after 11 years.

I cleaned the data, wrote six SQL queries, and recommended reviewing junior overtime workloads and adding a first-year programme.
